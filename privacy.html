Privacy Policy

Neptune - Fitness & Nutrition. Effective November 8, 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how Jacob Paluso ("we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, shares and protects your information when you use Neptune - Fitness & Nutrition, its watch apps and widgets, and the services behind them (the "Service"). It is part of our Terms of Service.

The short version: we collect what you log and what you allow the App to read from Apple Health or Health Connect, so the App can show it to you, back it up and share it with the people you choose. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, we do not use your health data for advertising or marketing, and the App contains no analytics or advertising trackers.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address and password (stored by our authentication provider in hashed form), or the name and email your Apple or Google account shares when you sign in with them. If you turn on two-step verification, the authenticator's secret is held by our authentication provider.
  • Profile: name, photo, birthday, sex, height, weight, activity level and your goals.
  • Health and fitness logs: foods, recipes, meals, meal photos, calories and nutrients, water, workouts, exercises, sets, routines, schedules, personal bests, sleep, weight and other body measurements, and notes.
  • Coaching and messages: your trainer link, chat messages and reactions, group chats, appointments, challenges, weekly feedback, and organization details if you are a trainer.
  • Support: feedback, issue reports, their photos and replies, and the diagnostic information described in Section 6.

Information from your devices, with your permission

  • Apple Health or Health Connect: the data types you allow, such as steps, active and resting energy, workouts, heart rate, sleep, weight, body measurements and other health metrics. You choose which types the App can read and write in your phone's settings, and you can change that at any time. If you allow it, the App also writes what you log in the App to Health: workouts, sleep, water, weight and other body measurements, and the nutrition of the foods you log.
  • Watch apps and sensors: while a workout is running, a paired Apple Watch, Wear OS watch or Bluetooth heart rate sensor sends the App live heart rate, energy, distance and steps. The App keeps the name and id of the sensor you chose on your phone only.
  • Camera and photos: only when you choose to take or pick a photo, scan a barcode or scan a nutrition label. Label scanning reads the text on your phone and does not send the image anywhere. Photos are re-encoded before upload, which removes camera metadata such as location.
  • Device information: your device model, operating system and app version, a push notification token, and settings such as your display preferences. We do not collect your precise location. (Older Android versions require a location permission to scan for Bluetooth sensors; the App does not use it to find your location.)

Information from others

  • A trainer you have connected with can add or change entries and goals for you; each change is recorded in your account's change history.
  • fatsecret returns food and recipe data for your searches.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information only to:

  • provide the Service: show your logs, totals, trends, goals, badges, streaks, widgets, watch screens and reminders;
  • back up and sync your data between your devices;
  • share your data with the people you choose, such as your trainer;
  • send the notifications you have turned on;
  • keep the Service and your account secure, prevent abuse and enforce our Terms;
  • answer your questions and fix the problems you report; and
  • comply with the law.

We do not use your health data for advertising, marketing or data mining, we do not sell it or share it with data brokers, and we do not use it to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects. We do not use your data to train artificial intelligence models.

3. Who We Share Information With

People you choose

  • Your trainer. Only after you agree to the Coaching Data Sharing Consent in the App. Your trainer can see your profile, logs, goals, health metrics and meal photos and can make changes for you, until you remove them.
  • Group chat and organization members. Your name and photo are shown to the other members of chats you join.
  • Share links. When you share an item, anyone signed in to the App who has the link can see it and your display name.

Service providers who process data on our behalf, only to run the Service, under contracts or terms that require them to protect it:

  • Supabase: account sign-in, database, file storage and server functions (where your data is stored).
  • Cloudflare (R2 storage): photos and data copies attached to issue reports.
  • GitHub: the private tracker where issue reports are filed.
  • Resend: delivers the feedback you send us by email.
  • Google (Gmail): delivers sign-up and password reset codes to your email address.
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and Apple (Push Notification service): deliver push notifications, including a short preview of a chat message if you have previews on.
  • fatsecret: receives the text you search for, barcodes you scan and the ids of foods and recipes you open, with no account details. Pictures of foods and recipes load directly from fatsecret's servers, which see your device's IP address.
  • Apple and Google sign-in: when you choose to use them.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy, and we require that they protect it at least as well as this policy does.

Legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information if the law requires it, to respond to valid legal process, to protect anyone's safety, or to investigate fraud, security problems or a breach of our Terms. Where the law allows, we will tell you first.

Business changes. If the Service is sold, merged or transferred, your information may be transferred as part of it, and this policy (or one at least as protective) will continue to apply. We will tell you before that happens.

We do not share your information with anyone else unless you ask us to.

4. Consumer Health Data

This section is our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy under the Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada's consumer health data law and similar laws.

  • Categories we collect: body measurements (weight, height, body fat and similar), food and nutrition intake, water, physical activity and workouts, heart rate and other vital signs your devices share, sleep, goals related to health, and any health conditions you mention in notes or messages. Where these come from Apple Health or Health Connect, we collect only the types you allow.
  • Sources: you, your devices and Apple Health or Health Connect, your trainer (for changes they make), and fatsecret (for food data you choose).
  • Purposes: only those in Section 2, and only those needed to provide the Service you asked for.
  • Sharing: only with the people and service providers in Section 3. We do not sell consumer health data.
  • Consent: we ask for your consent to collect and use your health data when you create your account, and separately before any of it is shared with a trainer. You can withdraw consent at any time (Section 7), which may stop parts of the Service from working.
  • Your rights: see Section 7, including the right to see a list of every third party and service provider we have shared your health data with. We do not geofence health care facilities.

5. How Long We Keep Information

  • Health and fitness logs (food, water, workouts, sleep, health metrics) are kept for 365 days, on your device and on our servers, except workouts holding a personal best, which are kept until you delete them. Daily presence records used for streaks are kept on your device for 4 years.
  • Meal photos are deleted after 14 days.
  • Profile, goals, saved foods, recipes, meals, routines, settings and badges are kept until you delete them or your account.
  • Chat messages are kept until the trainer link is removed, the chat is deleted or the organization is deleted. A message you delete is removed right away.
  • Share links expire 365 days after the item was last shared.
  • Push notification tokens are removed when you sign out, and unused ones after 90 days.
  • The diagnostic log on your device is kept for 30 days and never leaves the device unless you send it.
  • Issue reports: the photos and data copy attached to a report are deleted when the report is closed and in any case after 30 days; the link to the data copy expires after 7 days. A report with no activity for 30 days is closed automatically. The report text itself is kept so we can follow up.
  • Feedback you send us is kept until it is no longer needed or you delete your account; a copy also stays in the email we received.
  • Account deletion permanently deletes your account and the data linked to it from our servers right away. The exceptions are issue reports already filed in our private issue tracker (their attached photos and data copies are still deleted within 30 days), feedback emails we already received, and anything the law requires us to keep. Copies a trainer made outside the App are their responsibility. Backups held by our providers are overwritten on their normal schedule.

6. Diagnostics and Support

There is no analytics or crash-reporting software in the App. The App keeps a diagnostic log on your device to help fix problems. Email addresses, passwords and tokens are removed from it before it is ever sent. When you report an issue or email support from the App, you can include that log and a copy of your own data on the device (other accounts that have used the same device are removed from the copy). The App tells you what is attached before you send it.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • See and export your data: Settings > Device > Data & Storage > Your Data exports your account as CSV, JSON or a database file.
  • Correct your data: edit any entry, your profile or your goals in the App.
  • Delete your data: delete individual entries in the App, or delete your whole account from Settings by tapping your profile card and then Delete Account. Deleting data from the App does not delete what Apple Health or Health Connect already holds; manage that in the Health app or Health Connect.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off Health access in your phone's settings, remove your trainer (the Trainer button at the top of the App), turn off notifications, or delete your account. Withdrawing consent does not affect what happened before.
  • Request information: ask us what personal data we hold about you, who we have shared it with (including a list of the third parties and service providers that received your health data), or to delete it, using the contact details below.

Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Nevada, Texas, Virginia or Washington), you may have more rights, such as to know, access, correct, delete or move your data, to limit the use of sensitive data, to opt out of sale or targeted advertising (we do neither), and to appeal our decision on a request. We will not treat you differently for using your rights. We will verify a request by confirming it comes from the email address on the account, and answer within 45 days (or the time your state's law sets). If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our answer; if the appeal is denied, you may contact your state attorney general. An authorized agent may make a request for you with your signed permission.

8. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit, access rules on every table that let only you (and a trainer you approved) reach your data, encryption of chat text where it is stored, secure storage on your device for your sign-in session and password, optional two-step verification, and limits on how often sensitive actions can be tried. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your health information, we will notify you, and the Federal Trade Commission or other authorities where the law requires it, without unreasonable delay.

9. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and the App does not let anyone under 13 set up a profile. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it. Teenagers aged 13 to 17 may use the Service only with a parent or guardian's consent.

10. Where Data Is Processed

The Service is offered in the United States, and your information is processed in the United States and in other places where our service providers operate, under the protections described above.

11. Changes to This Policy

If we make a material change, we will ask you to review and accept the new policy in the App before you continue, and change the date at the top. We will not use your health data in a new way without asking for your consent first.
