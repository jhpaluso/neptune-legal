Delete Your Data

Neptune - Fitness & Nutrition

You can delete some or all of your data in Neptune - Fitness & Nutrition (developer: Jacob Paluso) without deleting your account. Everything below is deleted from your device and from our servers right away.

Delete All Your Logged Data

  • Open the app, go to Settings and tap your profile card.
  • Tap Delete Log Data and confirm.
  • This permanently deletes every food, water, workout, sleep and health entry you logged or synced into Neptune. Your profile, goals, saved foods, recipes, meals, exercises and routines are kept.

Delete Individual Items

  • A food, drink, workout, sleep or health entry: open it in the app and choose Delete.
  • Saved foods, recipes, meals, exercises and routines: open the item, tap its ⋮ menu and choose Delete.
  • A meal photo: remove it from the meal. Meal photos are also deleted automatically after 14 days.
  • A chat message you sent: press and hold it, then choose Delete.
  • Your trainer's access: remove your trainer from the My Trainer page (the Trainer button at the top of the app). This also deletes your messages and change history with them.

What Is Deleted and What Is Kept

  • Deleted right away when you delete it: the logged entries, items, photos and messages above, from your device and our servers.
  • Deleted automatically: health and fitness logs after 365 days (except workouts holding a personal best), meal photos after 14 days, the diagnostic log on your device after 30 days.
  • Kept until you delete your account: your profile, goals, settings and badges, unless you delete them yourself.
  • Kept after deletion: issue reports you already sent us stay in our private issue tracker (their attached photos and data copies are deleted within 30 days), and feedback emails we already received. Backups held by our hosting providers are overwritten on their normal schedule.

Data Kept by Apple Health or Health Connect

Neptune cannot delete data from Apple Health or Health Connect. Manage it in the Health app on iPhone or in Health Connect on Android. If Health sync is still on, Neptune may copy data that Health still holds back in.

Ask Us to Delete Data

Email NeptuneFitness@hotmail.com from the email address on your account and say what you would like deleted. We will reply within 45 days.

Delete Your Whole Account

To delete your account and all of its data, open the app, go to Settings, tap your profile card, then Delete Account. The Privacy Policy explains what is kept and for how long.
