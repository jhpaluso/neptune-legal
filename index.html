Delete Your Account

To delete your Neptune - Fitness & Nutrition account (developer: Jacob Paluso), open the app, go to Settings, tap your profile card, then Delete Account and confirm. Your account and all of its data (profile, logs, photos, saved foods, recipes, routines, goals, messages and trainer links) are deleted from our servers right away. Issue reports already sent to us stay in our private issue tracker (their attached files are deleted within 30 days), and feedback emails we already received are kept. You can also email NeptuneFitness@hotmail.com from the address on your account to ask us to delete it and its data. To delete only some of your data, see Delete your data.
