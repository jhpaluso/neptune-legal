Coaching Data Sharing Consent

Neptune - Fitness & Nutrition. Effective November 8, 2026.

Connecting with a trainer in Neptune - Fitness & Nutrition shares your health and fitness data with them. This consent explains exactly what is shared, what they can do, and how to stop it. It adds to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

What Your Trainer Can See

While you are connected, your trainer can see:

  • your profile: name, photo, birthday, sex, height, weight and activity level;
  • everything you log: foods, meals, meal photos, recipes, water, workouts, exercises and sets, sleep, weight and other health measurements, including what the App reads from Apple Health or Health Connect;
  • your goals, schedules, streaks, personal bests, challenges and progress; and
  • messages, appointments and feedback between the two of you.

Other trainers in your trainer's organization do not see your data. Your trainer's private notes and tags about you are visible only to them.

What Your Trainer Can Do

Your trainer can set and change your calorie, macro, water, sleep, step, weight and workout goals, add or change entries in your logs, share workouts and routines with you, set challenges, book appointments, invite you to group chats and send you messages. Every change they make to your account is recorded in your change history, which you can see in the App.

What You Should Know

  • Your trainer is independent. They are not employed, screened, certified or supervised by us, and we do not check their qualifications. Their advice is not medical advice, and we are not responsible for it. Talk to a doctor before starting a new program.
  • Your trainer may keep notes outside the App. Our Trainer Terms require them to use your data only to coach you and to keep it confidential, but what they do outside the App is their responsibility.
  • Health data is sensitive. It can reveal things about your health, body and habits. Only connect with someone you trust.
  • This is your choice. You do not have to connect with a trainer to use the App.

How to Stop Sharing

You can withdraw this consent at any time by removing your trainer from the My Trainer page (the Trainer button at the top of the App). Removing them stops all access right away and deletes your messages and change history with them. Data they already saw or copied before you removed them cannot be taken back by us. Deleting your account also ends every connection.

Agreement

By tapping Accept, you consent to share the data described above with the trainer named on this screen, for as long as you stay connected, and you confirm that you are at least 18 or that your parent or guardian agrees to this sharing.
